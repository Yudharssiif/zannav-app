How ZanNav Works

Get seamless transit directions across Unguja in three intuitive steps.

01

Set Origin & Destination

Use your current GPS location, tap any stop or point on the interactive island map, or open a location link sent to you on WhatsApp.

02

Get Multi-Modal Guidance

ZanNav identifies your closest boarding stop, guides your walk, informs you which Daladala number to board, and pinpoints where to alight or transfer.

03

Travel 100% Offline

No mobile data required. All routes, stops, road networks, and trip planning algorithms function completely on your local device.

ZanNav currently covers Unguja, Zanzibar, including key transit hubs and corridors such as Mnazi Mmoja, Fuoni, Mwanakwerekwe and Kiembe Samaki.
ZanNav uses an on-device multi-modal routing engine based on OpenStreetMap transit relations. When no direct bus connects your origin and destination, the engine searches intersecting corridors to recommend the optimal transfer stop (such as Makada), including instructions to walk and cross to the opposite stop.
ZanNav registers as an operating-system navigation provider for geographic links (`geo:` URIs and web map links). When you tap a location pin in WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS, simply choose ZanNav from the "Open with..." dialog; the app will automatically launch, set the destination, and calculate your route.
Inside the ZanNav app, navigate to Settings and tap "Report Missing Bus Stop". You can pin the stop location on the map, provide its local name, and submit it anonymously. Our team reviews submissions and contributes verified transit nodes back to OpenStreetMap.
