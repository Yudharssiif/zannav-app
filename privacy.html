Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026 • Unguja, Zanzibar

ZanNav ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by GeoNovex, is committed to respecting and protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent approach to data collection, usage, and local on-device processing for users in Unguja, Zanzibar.

Privacy-by-Design: ZanNav is built with an offline-first architecture. We do not require accounts, we do not sell your personal data, and we do not track your location on remote servers.

1. Information We Collect

A. Location Data (GPS Coordinates)

  • Purpose: With your permission, ZanNav accesses your device's approximate and precise location coordinates to identify nearby Daladala boarding stops, calculate walking directions, and center the map on your position.
  • On-Device Only: Location calculation and route matching are performed entirely on your device. Your live coordinates are never transmitted to remote servers or logged in tracking databases.
  • No Background Tracking: ZanNav does not track your location when the app is closed or minimized.

B. Community Contributions

If you voluntarily submit missing bus stop suggestions or report issues via the app, we collect the details you provide (stop name, category, and notes). These submissions are anonymous and contain no personal identity information.

C. Local Preferences

We store user settings locally on your phone (via Hive encrypted local storage), including your chosen language (Swahili or English), theme preference (Dark or Light), and downloaded offline map pack status.

2. Third-Party Endpoints

ZanNav accesses open map resources strictly to deliver map tiles and transit routes:

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM) & Overpass API: Used to download geographic vectors and public transport routes under the OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy.
  • No Advertising SDKs: ZanNav contains no third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or data brokers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all route queries, search caches, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device, you have complete control over your data.

To erase all data: Simply go to your phone's Settings > Apps > ZanNav > Storage and tap Clear Storage / Clear Data, or uninstall the app. All local databases and downloaded map packages will be permanently removed.

4. Children's Privacy

ZanNav is suitable for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated revision date.
