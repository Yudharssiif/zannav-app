# Privacy Policy for ZanNav

**Last updated:** September 24, 2026

**ZanNav** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by **GeoNovex**, is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and data when you use the ZanNav application in Unguja, Zanzibar.

We built ZanNav with an **offline-first and privacy-by-design** architecture: we do not sell your personal information, we do not require account registration, and your navigation queries remain on your device.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data (Precise & Approximate)
- **What is collected:** With your permission, ZanNav accesses your device's GPS and network-based location coordinates (`latitude` and `longitude`).
- **How it is used:** Location coordinates are used solely on your device to:
  1. Determine your current location on the island map;
  2. Identify the closest Daladala boarding stops;
  3. Calculate walking paths and transfers to destination points;
  4. Provide live location centering on the map.
- **Background Tracking:** ZanNav **does not** continuously track your location in the background when the application is closed or not in active use.
- **No Remote Tracking:** Your live location is processed locally on your device and is **never** transmitted to our servers or stored in remote tracking databases.

### B. User-Submitted Reports & Feedback
- If you voluntarily submit a missing bus stop or report a transit problem via the "Report a Stop" or "Report Issue" screens, we collect the details you provide (e.g., stop name, issue category, descriptive comments).
- **No Personal Identifiers:** These submissions are anonymous and do not require personal identification, phone numbers, or passwords.

### C. Local Preferences & Storage
- We store user preferences locally on your device via encrypted local storage (Hive), including:
  - Language selection (Kiswahili or English);
  - Interface theme (Light or Dark mode);
  - Offline map package download status;
  - Cached transit routes and stop locations.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information strictly to:
- Deliver public transit navigation and routing across Zanzibar;
- Facilitate offline browsing of Daladala lines, stops, and schedules;
- Improve the accuracy of bus stop locations based on community feedback;
- Maintain app stability and performance.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Open Data

ZanNav communicates with the following external endpoints strictly for essential map functionality:

- **OpenStreetMap (OSM) & Overpass API:** Map tile data, street geometry, and public transport relations are retrieved from OpenStreetMap foundation servers and Overpass API instances. These requests receive standard HTTP network logs (such as IP address) required for web communication as governed by the [OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **No Advertising Trackers:** ZanNav contains **no** third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no data broker integrations.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. We will only disclose information if required to do so by applicable law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- All routing histories, cached maps, and preferences reside entirely in your device's local storage.
- **How to delete your data:** You can instantly delete all data stored by ZanNav at any time by:
  1. Going to your device's **Settings > Apps > ZanNav > Storage**;
  2. Tapping **Clear Data / Clear Storage**; or
  3. Uninstalling the ZanNav application from your device.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

ZanNav is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.
